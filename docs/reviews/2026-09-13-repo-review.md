# Deep review of the repository — 2026-09-13

A one-pass, read-only review of `machxi-starter` at commit `28fa5d6` (after D19 shipped), covering architecture and patterns, library usage against current documentation, stack currency, and gaps in security, scalability and consistency. It is written to be worked from: every finding has an id, a severity, the evidence, why it matters and the smallest fix, so a step can cite `SEC-03` and close it.

**How it was done.** Static reading of the code, records, specs and tooling; `pnpm check` (green, 50 tasks), `pnpm test:scripts` (one failure, see PRO-01), `pnpm outdated -r`, `pnpm audit`, the two SPAs' `size` scripts against their committed `dist/`, and a file-by-file similarity pass over the two apps. Nothing was run against the operator's stack; Postman (`test:postman`) and the browser tier were not executed. No file outside `docs/reviews/` was changed.

**Severity.** `High` — fix before the next fork or production deployment. `Medium` — plan into a deliverable. `Low` — opportunistic, or bundle with nearby work. `Info` — a fact worth knowing, no action implied. Effort is `S` (an hour or two), `M` (a step), `L` (a deliverable).

## 1. Overall assessment

This is an unusually well-built blueprint. The API's security posture is well above what a starter normally ships: refresh rotation with reuse detection, CSRF double-submit per population, `__Host-`/`__Secure-` cookie prefixes, hashed single-use tokens consumed atomically inside transactions, PKCE + nonce + `azp` checks on the Google flow, a bounded argon2 pool, IP validation before `inet` and limiter keys, a database role split with an append-only trigger, and production refinements in the config schema that refuse every committed secret and default. The layering rules are enforced by lint rather than by review, the specs name their tests, the digests regenerate byte-identically, and the gate is the same command locally and in CI.

The findings below are therefore mostly about **the next scale up**, not about defects:

1. **Duplication between the two SPAs is the largest structural debt.** 58 file pairs are ≥80% identical (providers, auth and profile features, sidebar, test harness); a third consumer would be a third copy (FE-01).
2. **The record set has grown into chains.** 61% of the 72 ADRs are superseded by deltas, 13 records have two or more successors, and the truth of "sessions" now spans six records. The digests hide this from a session; they do not hide it from a fork (PRO-02).
3. **Single-instance and single-secret assumptions remain** where a SaaS would want otherwise: one HS256 secret with no rotation path, no scheduler for the prune, no RLS (designed in, deferred), blocking index builds inside one migration transaction (SEC-01, DB-01, DB-02, DB-03).
4. **Runtime advisories are two packages away from clean** (`nodemailer` 8 → 9.1.1+, `qs` 6.16.0), the build tool `tsdown` is pinned eleven minors behind since the initial commit, and a wave of majors (ESLint 10, Vite 8, Vitest 5, TypeScript 7) is queued in Renovate (STK-01…STK-04).
5. **A few claims have drifted from the code**: two skills still say `apps/web` transpiles `@repo/ui`, the CLAUDE.md size test fails and nothing in the gate runs it, and three routes have no Postman request (PRO-01, PRO-03, PRO-04).

## 2. Architecture and patterns (API)

The API follows its own rules. `routes → controller → service → repository` holds everywhere (`packages/config-eslint/api.js` refuses Express in services and repositories, schema tables outside repositories, `res.json` outside `core/http`, classes, `this`, decorators and `process.env` outside `core/config`); `process.env` appears in exactly the three places the digest names. Validation goes through `req.valid` (`core/middleware/validate.ts`), errors through one `AppError` (`core/middleware/error-handler.ts`), the envelope through `respond.*`. No `TODO`/`FIXME` markers exist in the tree; ten `eslint-disable` comments exist in ~70k lines.

- **API-01 · `Medium` · The composition root has become a 909-line hand-wired file.** `apps/api/src/core/container.ts` builds ~20 rate limiters, every repository, service and controller by hand, and every new flow adds another block (the last three deliverables each added limiters, a service and a controller there). _Why:_ it is the file every step touches, so it is where merge conflicts between a fork and `starter/main` will land first. _Fix (M):_ split by concern — `createRateLimiters(config, store)` returning the named set, and one `wire<Module>(deps)` per module returning `{ repositories, services, controllers }` — keeping `buildContainer` as the ordering only. **Closed at `D20.6`.**
- **API-02 · `Medium` · Four services exceed 1,000 lines.** `modules/auth/auth.service.ts` 2,187, `modules/organizations/organizations.service.ts` 1,211, `modules/users/users.service.ts` 1,209, `modules/staff/staff.service.ts` 1,036, `modules/users/users.repository.ts` 1,033. The auth service alone holds login, refresh, logout, registration, verification, recovery, onboarding, email change, impersonation issue and federated exchange. _Why:_ the file is cohesive by aggregate but no longer by reader; the federated and impersonation halves already live in sibling files, which is the precedent. _Fix (M):_ move recovery/verification/email-change (`ADR-0071`) into `auth.mail-flows.service.ts` behind the same `createAuthService` deps, and the personal-organization glue out of `users.service.ts` into a small `users.lifecycle.ts`. **Closed at `D20.7`.**
- **API-03 · `Low` · Comment density hides the code.** Route files are more than half prose citing step numbers (`auth.routes.ts` is 530 lines for 25 routes; `users.routes.ts` 353 for 16). `ADR-0065` deliberately keeps step references in code, so this is a judgment call, not a violation. _Why:_ a reviewer scanning for a missing guard reads paragraphs between registrations; a fork inherits another repository's biography in every file. _Fix (S, ongoing):_ keep the one-line record citation at each route and move the "why the reviewer said so" narrative into the capability's spec or the plan's `.notes.md`. **Deferred — §Not in D20 of the plan: ongoing, a route file at a time; no row.**
- **API-04 · `Low` · No server-level request timeouts.** `apps/api/src/server.ts` calls `app.listen` with Node's defaults (`headersTimeout` 60 s, `requestTimeout` 300 s, no `keepAliveTimeout` tuning). The reverse proxy in the reference deployment bounds this, a direct exposure does not. _Fix (S):_ set `server.requestTimeout`, `headersTimeout` and `keepAliveTimeout` from config beside `DEFAULT_SHUTDOWN_TIMEOUT_MS`. **Closed at `D20.4`.**
- **API-05 · `Low` · Mail is sent in the request path with no outbox.** `core/mail/delivery.ts` swallows failures (`deliver`) or detaches the send (`deliverDetached`) by decision (`ADR-0071 §3–§4`); a message lost between the response and the relay is "recoverable by asking again". _Why:_ correct for the enumeration argument, but a deployment with a flaky relay silently loses onboarding and invitation mail, and there is no retry or dead-letter. _Fix (M, later):_ an `outbox` table written in the same transaction as the token, drained by the scheduler DB-02 asks for; the port stays the same. **Routed to D24 (the outbox).**
- **API-06 · `Info` · No OpenAPI; Postman is the contract.** `docs/README.md` says "until OpenAPI exists". Zod 4 ships `z.toJSONSchema`, so generating an OpenAPI document from `@repo/contracts` is now a small script rather than a dependency. Deferred by `ADR-0021`; worth a step when a second client (D16, mobile) arrives. **Deferred — §Not in D20 of the plan; no row.**
- **API-07 · `Info` · No response compression on the API.** The SPAs are gzipped by nginx; list responses from the API are not. Fine behind a compressing proxy; note for a direct exposure. **Deferred — §Not in D20 of the plan; no row.**

## 3. Security

Verified controls are listed in §10 so the findings can be read in proportion. Nothing below is a vulnerability that is exploitable in the reference deployment; they are hardening items and single-instance assumptions.

- **SEC-01 · `Medium` · One shared HS256 secret with no rotation path.** `modules/auth/auth.tokens.ts:45–46` pins `HS256` and signs with `JWT_ACCESS_SECRET`; `core/oidc/transaction.ts` seals the OIDC transaction with the **same** secret (audience-separated). The comment promises "the future secret-rotation ADR" and no such record exists. _Why:_ rotating the secret is a global sign-out and a broken in-flight Google sign-in; a leaked secret cannot be retired gracefully. _Fix (M):_ a key ring — `JWT_ACCESS_SECRETS` as current+previous with a `kid` header, verify against both, sign with current — or an EdDSA key pair; and a separate secret for the OIDC transaction codec so the two can rotate independently. A new record supersedes `ADR-0018` on this point only. **Routed to D23.**
- **SEC-02 · `Medium` · No per-principal budget on expensive authenticated routes.** `change-password`, `DELETE /user/profile`, `DELETE /identities/:provider` and the admin `POST /:id/password` routes draw on the shared argon2 pool under the global per-IP limiter only; the routes' own comments record it as an accepted risk (`users.routes.ts` ~170–200, `staff.routes.ts` ~185–200, `ADR-0020 §Residual risks`, `ADR-0027 §Consequences`). _Why:_ a handful of authenticated clients can make the hash limiter shed, and what it sheds is login for everyone. `POST /admin/{staff,users}/:id/password` is additionally a guessing oracle on the actor's own password. _Fix (S):_ a `createPrincipalRateLimiter` keyed on `ctx.principal.id` (the impersonation limiter already has the shape) mounted on the five routes; it closes both the pool and the oracle. **Closed at `D20.4`.**
- **SEC-03 · `Low` · Limiter key degrades to a shared bucket under a wrong proxy hop.** `core/middleware/context.ts` sets `ctx.ip` only when `req.ip` is a real address; `core/middleware/rate-limit.ts:96` then keys on `ipKeyGenerator(req.ctx.ip ?? "")`. With a mis-set `TRUST_PROXY` every caller shares the `""` bucket and the global limiter refuses everyone after 300 requests. _Fix (S):_ fall back to `req.socket.remoteAddress`, or refuse the request with a logged configuration error; either is better than a silent shared bucket. **Closed at `D20.4`.**
- **SEC-04 · `Low` · No Content-Security-Policy on the SPAs.** `apps/admin/nginx.conf` and `apps/customer/nginx.conf` restate helmet's headers and say in place that CSP is omitted because Tailwind emits inline styles. A `style-src 'unsafe-inline'` policy with a strict `script-src 'self'` and `frame-ancestors 'none'` is still worth having: it is script injection the policy would stop, not styles. _Fix (S):_ add that policy to both nginx files; revisit nonces if a build step ever emits inline scripts. **Closed at `D20.8`.**
- **SEC-05 · `Low` · No cap on concurrent sessions per principal; pruning is manual.** `sessions` grows one row per login and shrinks only when `pnpm db:sessions:prune` is run (`packages/db/src/prune-sessions.ts:14–21`: "no scheduler yet"). A credential-stuffing run against a known account leaves thousands of live rows. _Fix (S):_ a soft cap (e.g. revoke the oldest beyond N per principal on login) plus DB-02. **Closed at `D20.4` (the cap) and `D20.5` (the `prune` service); the in-process scheduler is routed to D24.**
- **SEC-06 · `Low` · GitHub Actions are pinned by major tag, not SHA.** `.github/workflows/gate.yml` and `e2e.yml` use `actions/checkout@v4`, `pnpm/action-setup@v4`, `actions/setup-node@v4`, `actions/cache@v4`, `actions/upload-artifact@v4`. _Fix (S):_ pin to commit SHAs and let Renovate (`helpers:pinGitHubActionDigests`) keep them moving. **Closed at `D20.2`.**
- **SEC-07 · `Low` · Tenant isolation is code-enforced only.** No `ROW LEVEL SECURITY` exists in `packages/db/migrations`; the repository parameter plus the roster test (`packages/db/src/__tests__/enumerations.test.ts`) are the enforcement. `ADR-0067 §5` designs RLS in and defers it "with its preconditions written down" — and D14's role split has since met the precondition. Not a gap against the record; listed here because it is the one control that would make a forgotten `organization_id` a database error rather than a review finding. _Fix (L):_ a deliverable: `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` on the tenant-owned tables, a policy over `current_setting('app.organization_id', true)`, `SET LOCAL` in the membership guard's transaction, and the enumeration test extended to assert a policy per table. **Routed to D25.**
- **SEC-08 · `Info` · Verified as safe, recorded so nobody re-checks.** Google flow: handoff token bound to the browser by a sealed SHA-256 in the cookie (`transaction.ts` `sealHandoff`), `state` compared in constant time, `azp` required on multi-audience tokens, `email_verified` and "authoritative" domain rules (`google-oidc-provider.ts`). Impersonation: new tab pre-opened on the click and `tab.opener = null` (`apps/admin/src/features/users-manage/impersonate-user-dialog.tsx:184–227`). Client: no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, `escapeValue: false` is correct under React (`packages/i18n/src/create-i18n.ts:297`), the only `sessionStorage` write is a fenced relative path (`apps/customer/src/features/auth/google-sign-in/redirect-memory.ts`). Logs: query strings stripped, four request fields serialised, addresses never logged on refusal paths. **Nothing to close — an `Info` observation, re-read at `D20.13` and still true.**

## 4. Data layer

- **DB-01 · `Medium` · Index builds are blocking and inside one migration transaction.** `migrations/0010_list_indexes.sql` header says so deliberately (`ADR-0060 §5` rule 2); Drizzle's `migrate()` wraps the pending batch in one transaction, so `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY` is impossible through `src/migrate.ts`. _Why:_ the first fork with a populated `users` table takes a write lock for the length of a trigram build. _Fix (M):_ a documented second lane — a migration file whose statements the runner executes outside the transaction (a `-- concurrently` marker the runner honours), or a small `db:index` tool for that class of change. Record it as a delta on `ADR-0060`. **Routed to D25 (the concurrent-index lane).**
- **DB-02 · `Medium` · There is no scheduler; every retention job is a CLI.** `prune-sessions.ts` (sessions, handoffs, account tokens, 30-day retention, batched), invitation expiry (a state derived from `expires_at`, never swept by decision, `packages/db/src/schema/organizations.ts` ~175), audit retention ("a deployment decision", `schema/audit.ts:23–30`). _Why:_ the reference deployment (`docker/prod/compose.yaml`) has no service that ever runs the prune, so a production database grows until someone remembers. _Fix (S now, M later):_ now — a `prune` service in the prod compose on a cron-style restart, or the platform's scheduled job, documented in `docs/deploy.md`; later — an in-process scheduler seam (`ADR-0066`'s shape) that also drains API-05's outbox. **Closed at `D20.5` for the deployment half (the `prune` service); the scheduler and audit retention are routed to D24.**
- **DB-03 · `Low` · `updated_at` is maintained by hand.** `schema/_shared.ts:52–58` documents "written by the repository on every update — no trigger". Drizzle 0.45 supports `.$onUpdate(() => new Date())` on the column, which makes a forgotten write impossible without a trigger. _Fix (S):_ add `$onUpdate` to `timestamps()`; the repositories that set it explicitly keep working. **Closed at `D20.5`.**
- **DB-04 · `Low` · The "every active user has a proven address" invariant is code-only.** `schema/users.ts` `emailVerifiedAt` comment states the invariant everything downstream leans on (invitation acceptance by address, federated linking by `status` alone). _Fix (S):_ a `CHECK (status <> 'active' OR email_verified_at IS NOT NULL)` after a one-off backfill assertion; migration-sized. **Closed at `D20.5`.**
- **DB-05 · `Low` · Version pins.** `postgres:16-alpine` in both compose files and CI while 17 (2024) and 18 (2025) are current; `citext` and `pg_trgm` are the only extensions. No urgency; a fork on a managed 17/18 works today. Note that `ADR-0061`'s `REASSIGN OWNED BY` upgrade path is the only documented data-migration story — there is no backup/restore text anywhere beyond "managed Postgres". **Deferred — §Not in D20 of the plan; no row.**
- **DB-06 · `Info` · What is right.** UUIDv7 app-side keys; `citext` addresses with partial unique indexes so deletion releases an address; `(column, id)` composites matching the tiebreaker; tenant-led composites and no bare pairs on tenant tables; trigram GIN with the measured `::text` cast (`core/http/list-query.ts`); `LIKE` metacharacters escaped; `MAX_OFFSET` and `count=false`; advisory locks on both the migration run and the token mint; atomic single-use consumes; `TRUNCATE` withheld from the app role; the test harness running as the app role so a missing grant fails in CI; seeds refusing committed passwords in production. **Nothing to close — an `Info` observation, re-read at `D20.13` and still true.**

## 5. Frontend and platform packages

- **FE-01 · `High` · The two SPAs duplicate their composition and account features.** Of 115 comparable file paths, 58 are ≥80% identical after stripping comments and 33 more are 50–80%. The doors under `shared/` are per-app by design (`ADR-0051`); the rest are not:

  - `features/profile-manage/change-password-dialog.tsx` (96%), `profile-details-form.tsx` (84%);
  - `features/{theme,locale,density}` cards (100%), `shared/notifications/messages.ts` (100%), `shared/error-reporting/*` (100%), `shared/ui/query-error.tsx` (100%);

  `ADR-0062` extracted the layouts, loader, error boundary and router defaults into `@repo/app-kit` and stopped there. _Why:_ every fix to session boot, settings hydration or the reset flow is made twice and tested twice (the `__tests__` pairs are 62–100% similar too); D16 and any mobile shell would copy a third time. _Fix (L, one deliverable):_ move the four providers and the bootstrap into `@repo/app-kit` parameterised by segment (the customer's `session.tsx` differs only in copy and segment), the password-reset and profile-manage features and the three preference cards into a `@repo/account` feature package, and the MSW envelope/list-query helpers into `@repo/app-kit/testing`. Keep the sidebar and dashboard layout per app — those genuinely differ. **Routed to D22 — the MSW helpers, its third part, moved at `D20.10`.**

- **FE-02 · `Medium` · Bundle budgets are set 2–5% above measurement.** `apps/admin/size-budget.json` vs measured: js 353.9/371.1 kB, css 14.5/15.2, entry 163.3/171.9, react 59.0/63.5, router 27.9/30.3, table 13.7/14.8, query 10.3/11.2; customer js 302.5/309.6, entry 109.3/111.3. _Why:_ the gate's fifth link fails on the first screen a fork adds, and the answer will be to raise the number — which is the ratchet failing quietly. _Fix (S):_ decide the policy in the record (`ADR-0062 §4`): a fixed headroom (e.g. +15% over the last measured) with the measured value committed beside the ceiling, so a raise is a diff that shows both. **Closed at `D20.9`.**
- **FE-03 · `Medium` · The entry chunk carries too much.** 163 kB gzipped for the admin entry and 109 kB for the customer (with vendors already split out) means the grid, the form machinery, both locale catalogues and every door are in the first paint. `ADR-0062` chose route-level `autoCodeSplitting`; nothing is lazy below a route. _Fix (M):_ lazy-load the `lv` catalogue (i18next backend already in place via `i18next-resources-to-backend`), make `@repo/grid` a dynamic import behind the list pages, and measure — expect 30–40% off the entry. **Closed at `D20.9`.**
- **FE-04 · `Medium` · Platform-package behaviour is certified through the admin app.** `apps/admin/src/shared/grid/__tests__/app-grid.test.tsx` (1,839 lines) and `shared/form/__tests__/*` (~1,840 lines) test `@repo/grid` and `@repo/form` through the door; `packages/grid` and `packages/form` carry smaller suites (2,573 and 213 test lines). The customer app has no grid door at all. _Why:_ a fork that removes the admin app, or a package change made from the package's own suite, loses the certification the extraction (D9) left behind. _Fix (M):_ move the door-level suites into the packages (`@repo/app-kit/testing` already provides the harness), leaving one smoke test per door in the app. **Closed at `D20.10` for the harness; the door-level suites are routed to D22.**
- **FE-05 · `Low` · Two oversized modules.** `packages/grid/src/app-grid.tsx` (1,517 lines) holds state wiring, header rendering, body rendering, selection and density in one component; `packages/api-client/src/client.ts` (1,284 lines) holds the axios instance, interceptors, session lifecycle and twelve auth flows. _Fix (M):_ split the client into `transport.ts` (instance + interceptors), `session.ts` (refresh, bootstrap, end) and `flows/*.ts`; split the grid into header/body/state hooks. Behaviour-preserving, well-covered by tests. **Closed at `D20.7`.**
- **FE-06 · `Low` · No `prefers-reduced-motion` handling.** `tw-animate-css` animations run unconditionally; no `motion-reduce:` variant or media query exists in `packages/ui` or the apps. _Fix (S):_ a global `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rule in `globals.css` disabling animation and transition durations. **Closed at `D20.8`.**
- **FE-07 · `Info` · Library usage matches current documentation.** TanStack Table v9 through `useTable` + `tableFeatures` with no row models (`packages/grid/src/features.ts`); Query v5 with typed `meta` via `Register` augmentation and cache-level handlers (`packages/app-kit/src/providers/query-client.ts`); Router with `parseSearch`/`stringifySearch` and `defaultErrorComponent`; `zodResolver` from `@hookform/resolvers/zod` (correct for zod 4 with resolvers 5); i18next 26 with `CustomTypeOptions` per app and package namespaces kept off it; React 19 without `forwardRef`; Tailwind v4 `@theme`/`@source`/`@custom-variant`. `@dnd-kit/core` + `sortable` are the maintained line; `@dnd-kit/react` is a rewrite worth watching, not a migration to make. **Nothing to close — an `Info` observation, re-read at `D20.13` and still true.**
- **FE-08 · `Info` · Accessibility basics are present.** `role="alert"` on form errors, `role="status"`/`aria-live` on loaders, the applied-filters announcer and the banner; assertions by accessible name in tests; a pseudo-locale suite proving no literal survives. ## 7. Build, tooling, CI and deployment

- **CI-01 · `Medium` · The gate does not run everything that can go red.** `test:scripts` (the repo-level script tests) is red today (PRO-01) and appears in neither `gate.yml` nor the documented gate; `docs:check` and `test:postman` are likewise outside CI. _Fix (S):_ add `pnpm test:scripts` and `pnpm docs:check` to `pnpm check` and to the gate (both are seconds); leave Postman nightly beside e2e, where the stack exists. **Closed at `D20.1` (the two links run) and `D20.12` (the `check-types` link checks).**
- **CI-02 · `Low` · No test coverage is measured anywhere.** `packages/config-vitest/{node,react}.ts` configure no `coverage`; no threshold, no report. _Why:_ the suites are large (~53k lines) but nothing says which slices are thin — e.g. `packages/form` has 213 test lines against 2,150 source lines outside the admin door tests. _Fix (S):_ `coverage: { provider: "v8", reporter: ["text-summary"] }` in the shared configs and a per-package floor where it matters (contracts, api-client, crypto). **Closed at `D20.1` — coverage reported, not gated; a threshold is §Not in D20.**
- **CI-03 · `Low` · Version declarations are repeated across 18 manifests.** `typescript`, `vitest`, `eslint`, `@types/node`, `zod`, `react`, `react-dom`, `@tanstack/react-router`, `i18next`, `react-i18next`, `jsdom`, testing-library are each declared in 5–16 workspaces; three already drift (`tsx ^4.19.4` vs `^4.23.12`, `typescript 5.9.3` vs `^5.9.3`, `@testing-library/react ^16.3.2` vs `^16.3.0`). _Fix (S):_ pnpm `catalog:` in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` for the shared set; Renovate understands catalogs. **Closed at `D20.2`.**

## 10. What is done well

Listed so the findings above are read in proportion.

- Config validated before anything is built; every committed secret and every development default enumerated and refused in production (`core/config/schema.ts:731–831`); the database probed before the port binds; the dummy hash warmed before the first login; drain with a deadline and the pool closed after.
- Guards at the mount, narrowing per route, `super_admin` bypass on one greppable line; a third guard (`requireMembership`) that answers `404` to non-members and `403` to members; `refuseImpersonated` as a named, audited guard on the takeover list.
- Sessions: hashed refresh tokens, optimistic rotation (`where refresh_token_hash = expected`), a grace window with reuse detection that revokes, per-request session probe so revocation is immediate, impersonated sessions with a fixed end and the RFC 8693 `act` claim checked against the row.
- Cookies: `__Secure-` refresh scoped to the segment path, `__Host-` CSRF per population, `__Host-` OIDC transaction on `Lax`, duplicate-name cookies read as absent.
- Mail tokens: one mechanism for five flows, advisory-locked mint, supersession, atomic consume inside the caller's transaction, purpose and population in the `where`.
- The Google flow: discovery pinned to `https` and the issuer, `RS256` only, `exp`/`iat`/`sub` required, nonce, `azp` on multi-audience, a handoff bound to the browser that started it, account resolution in one transaction with a row lock.
- Enumeration resistance as a discipline: identical answers and detached sends on every anonymous path, addresses never logged on refusal.
- Roles: an application role with table privileges only, `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES` so new tables need no grant, a `BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE` trigger plus a revoked grant on `audit_events`, a post-condition in the migration that refuses a partial grant, the test suite running as the app role.
- Lint as the mechanical half of every record: layering, doors generated from a table, no literal JSX strings, no `process.env`, no classes, no raw responses, no schema tables outside repositories, no fake OIDC provider outside tests.
- The generated digests, the specs naming their tests, the executable acceptance checklists, and a gate that is one command in both places.
