**Logged by the token column's review, 2026-09-04.** `D15.3`: a Postman or dev fixture invitation must mint `sha256Base64url(randomToken())` and never a literal — a constant hash in a seed is one shared credential across every clone the day accept-by-token exists. Beyond D15, for the deliverable that ships the send: whether acceptance binds to the **bearer** of the token or to a verified address is the decision that actually closes this finding and deserves its own record; **resend and rotation** have no semantics yet (`not null` and unique mean a resend either re-sends the same token or rotates the hash); and **retention** — no sweep is planned, so a consumed or expired row keeps the invited address indefinitely, which is PII rather than a credential and wants the `db:sessions:prune` shape.
