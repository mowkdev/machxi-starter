# Admin app, design system and the platform packages (ADRs 0030–0054)

Loaded when you touch `apps/admin`, `apps/customer`, `packages/ui`, or any of the six packages D9 extracted. Read `/frontend-conventions` before writing code here; the ADRs are the source of truth. **ADRs 0001–0056 are frozen** — D11 shipped 2026-08-31 and D12 2026-09-01 — so a divergence from any of them is a superseding ADR, never an edit. **The D13 window is open (opened 2026-09-01)** over ADR-0057 (impersonation sessions) and ADR-0058 (the append-only audit log). **0057 is `accepted` — 2026-09-01 at `D13.1`'s head**, where 0018 moved to `superseded by` and 0052 took its second successor delta, the gate's fifth link: the gate is `pnpm lint && pnpm check-types && pnpm test && pnpm size && pnpm format:check` from that step on. **0058 is `accepted` — 2026-09-01 at `D13.2`'s head**, additive: the append-only `audit_events` table and its writer, superseding nothing. **The D11 window shut when D11 shipped 2026-08-31**; it covered ADR-0053 and ADR-0054 only. **0054 is `accepted` — 2026-08-30 at `D11.1`'s head**, where **0037 moved to `superseded by`, 0048 gained a second-successor delta and 0052 its first**; **0053 is `accepted` — 2026-08-30 at `D11.6`'s head**, where **0045 moved to `superseded by`** with two deltas (where the theme control renders; the settings core gains `gridDensity`). **The D12 window shut when D12 shipped 2026-09-01**; it covered ADR-0055 and, from 2026-09-01, ADR-0056 (the per-population CSRF cookie, accepted at `D12.12`'s head, where 0029 takes a one-name delta) — 0055 the customer app's record, **`accepted` 2026-08-31 at `D12.1`'s head**, where **0022 moved to `superseded by`** (a delta: the roster; every stack decision 0022 made stands). Check `docs/plan/README.md` rather than assuming. **0031 is superseded by 0047, 0032 by 0049, 0024 by 0050, 0045 by 0053, both 0033 and 0049 by 0051** (accepted 2026-08-27 at `D9.1`), **0039, 0048, 0026 and 0036 by 0052** — the last two at `D10.5`, a delta on one sentence each (the gate's fourth link), with every caching and testing rule in them standing — **and 0037, 0048 (again), 0052, 0038 and 0041 by 0054** — the last two at `D11.12`, a delta on where Create and the Columns control are drawn: they sit in the page-header band beside the density toggle, icon-only at 34×34, reached from `@repo/grid` through the portal `@repo/ui` publishes, and the grid toolbar has no right-hand group on any shipped screen. 0034 and 0040 are superseded too (by 0043 and 0044). A superseded record is out of force: read its successor — and where the supersession is a **delta** (0026, 0033, 0036, 0037, 0038, 0039, 0041, 0045, 0048, 0049, 0052) the parent still stands for everything the successor does not touch, which for 0048 means everything _neither_ of its two successors touches. **0022 joined that delta list at `D12.1`**, superseded by 0055 for the roster alone, **and 0018 and 0052 at `D13.1`** — 0018 by 0057 for impersonated sessions alone (every ordinary-session decision stands), and 0052 for the gate's fifth link, which makes it the second record here with two partial successors.

**Where the machinery lives since D9** (ADR-0051): `@repo/api-client` (client, session, CSRF, URL⇄query), `@repo/i18n` (the `createI18n` factory), `@repo/resource` (`ResourceConfig` and its readers), `@repo/grid`, `@repo/form` (including the zod locale installer), `@repo/app-services` (theme, grid density, `can()`, `route-meta`). Each app reaches them **only** through its `shared/` doors — `no-restricted-imports` refuses the package path anywhere else — and a door is the only path to those symbols, not necessarily one slice per package.

- **Design system** (0047, superseding 0031; the language is 0054): shadcn/ui in `@repo/ui` on Tailwind v4, reached only through the `shared/ui` door. **The design speaks through exactly three surfaces** (0054): the token sheet `packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css`, the hand-written compositions, and `@repo/grid`'s own styling — a need none of the three can meet is an owned edit with its comment or a finding for the ADR, never a quiet restyle of a generated primitive. Since `D11.1` the tokens are **"Instrument"**: IBM Plex Sans/Mono self-hosted through `@fontsource` and mapped in `@theme`, every neutral and the primary on hue 258–264 at chroma ≤ 0.014, `--radius: 6px` with the 4·5·6·8 scale, solid (never alpha) dark borders over a lifted floor, and four tokens beyond shadcn's set — `--primary-tint`, `--border-strong`, `--success`, `--warning`. **Colour, radius, type and spacing come from tokens; a raw hex or an arbitrary `oklch()` in a component is the defect.** Generated primitives under `packages/ui/src/components/` and `src/hooks/` stay byte-identical to the CLI output (prettier-exempt); an edit to one must earn itself with a comment naming the rule that forced it. **Hand-written compositions live in `packages/ui/src/compositions/`** under the ordinary code regime — formatted, tested in `src/__tests__/`, edited rather than regenerated — and are presentation-only: no `@repo/contracts` import, no routing, no forms, no session, and the dependency list does not grow for them. `@repo/ui` gains no i18n dependency — its operator-facing strings come through the `UiStrings` context (0046), which makes any composition with micro-copy a `"use client"` component.
- **Architecture** (0049, superseding 0032; superseded by 0051 for the `shared/` tree — those slices are doors over packages now): FSD layers `app → pages → widgets → features → entities → shared`, downward-only imports lint-enforced, TanStack Router file-based routes; an entity may not import another entity — the feature layer is where two entities meet. `widgets/dashboard-layout` is a thin adapter over `@repo/ui`'s `AppShell` and the account menu lives in the shell's header, not the sidebar footer (§The-shell-adapter).
- **Process** (0050, superseding 0024): a superseding ADR states the delta and carries the parent's untouched sections by reference; an ADR records decisions, not counts or enumerations a compiler could check; verification is the deterministic gate plus **one bounded review pass per step on the diff** — no refutation rounds.
- **Session** (0033, superseded by 0051 — the decisions stand, the code moved to `@repo/api-client`): credentials in memory only — no token, CSRF token or principal in `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or a client-set cookie; non-credential display state (theme, locale, grid preferences) may be persisted; single-flight refresh; CSRF via the 0030 bootstrap endpoint; the client never _reads_ `document.cookie`.
- **Server state** (0043): TanStack Query owns all server state; per-entity key factories, never an inline key; outcomes declared in typed mutation `meta` and delivered by one global handler; queries are silent and render their own failure panel. **List state lives in the URL in the API's own query language** — `?sort=-email&filter[status]=active` — through the one codec in `@repo/contracts`; a record URL carries only the record, and the way back travels as history state through `shared/grid/record-navigation` (0044).
- **Resources** (0035): one `ResourceConfig` per resource — serializable config plus named registries — for both `AppGrid` (TanStack Table v9, manual mode, over shadcn `Table`) and config-driven RHF forms over the contracts zod schemas; every field disabled while a mutation is pending. Copy in a config is a translation key (0046).
- **The v2 grid** (0037 as carried by 0054, 0038–0042, 0044, 0045): a grid page is a height budget with one scroll container, a sticky header and a static pager, every wrapper down to `AppGrid` carrying `flex flex-col flex-1 min-h-0` and nothing on that path clipping; `grid` capability flags say what a surface _offers_, `can(action, resource)` says who may use it, conjoined once per screen so `shared/grid` never learns a resource name; bulk delete is a bounded client-side fan-out with per-item refusals; no filter row — a filterable column gets a header dialog that commits on Apply, `grid.hotFilters` are toolbar stances, and the applied-filters bar renders from the URL, never from the configs. **The URL owns view state, preferences own ergonomics, history state owns the way back, the table owns nothing durable.** Preferences, theme, locale and — since `D11.6` — row density are an account fact in `settings` with `localStorage` as the cache in front; the store never learns a `gridId` is on the wire. **Density is a mode, not a scale** (0054 §6): Comfortable 44 / Compact 34, moving exactly the row height, the cell padding on `th` and `td`, and the in-row badge; `@repo/grid` takes it as a prop, `@repo/app-services` holds the account fact, and the account area is `/account` with sections (0053). **Since `D11.10` the account menu is identity and exit only** — the principal block, one link to `/account`, and sign out — the theme, density and locale controls live in `/account/preferences`, and the sidebar carries no account entry at all (0053 §2/§3).
- **Copy has two catalogues, both localized** (0046): error _codes_ in the `errors` i18n namespace (typed complete over `ErrorCode`), field _rules_ as zod's own messages through the client-side error map (`lv` map in `@repo/contracts/locales`); the API's `error.message` is never rendered. Every operator-facing string is a typed i18next key — a literal in JSX fails lint. `en` means en-GB; `lv` is machine-drafted pending native review.
- **Testing** (0036): Vitest + Testing Library + MSW with handlers derived from `@repo/contracts`; no live-API tier; a mock more permissive than the service certifies the opposite of production. Assertions by accessible name in English; the pseudo-locale suite proves no literal survived.
- **Invalidation**: a mutation invalidates its own entity's keys; the feature invalidates whatever else the interaction touched.
